About this model

The walk area is a grid of 5 m cells built from real elevation data (and, at Jezero, ground-hardness data). Each step between cells costs metabolic energy: walking speed follows a hiking law on the local slope and drops on loose sand and rough ground; power rises uphill, on sand and with a heavy kit; slopes over 20° are off limits. Energy becomes suit oxygen (about 20.1 kJ per litre of O2), with 20% of the tank held in reserve. The planner finds the least-effort path and the best order of stops, then adds radiation dose from the time spent outside, the point of no return, where to turn back if a dust storm starts, a 6-hour EVA time limit, the stretches where the habitat's antenna can't see the astronaut, and the best set of stops that fits. Every number is labelled as a published source, a proxy, an assumption or a design choice.

Data

  • Elevation: USGS Astrogeology HiRISE DTM mosaics, Gale (MSL) and Jezero (Mars 2020 TRN).
  • Ground hardness (Jezero only): THEMIS-derived thermal inertia of Jezero Crater (Zenodo, CC BY 4.0).
  • Imagery: NASA Mars Trek (MRO HiRISE / CTX) and the USGS Gale HiRISE colour mosaic; rover traverses from NASA/JPL MMGIS.
  • Gale route context: NASA's Curiosity's proposed path up Mount Sharp, the challenge's example map for the boxwork area.
  • Stop photos: Curiosity and Perseverance raw images, NASA/JPL-Caltech; each photo shows its own credit.
  • Weather: Curiosity REMS and Perseverance MEDA, both led by Spain's Centro de Astrobiología (CAB), from the NASA weather feed; dust optical depth from the Mars Climate Database (LMD / ESA / CNES) can be entered by hand (not filled in this snapshot).
  • Solar particle events: NASA CCMC DONKI, detected near Earth and used as a proxy for Mars.
  • Radiation: Curiosity RAD at Gale, Hassler et al. 2014 (~0.64 mSv/day; readable copy). Measured at Gale; used as a proxy at Jezero.

AI assistance

This project was built for NASA Space Apps 2026 with AI coding agents (Anthropic Claude and OpenAI Codex), each working in its own part of the code, with the team reviewing and merging the work. Files carry an AI-assistance note on their first line. The science calls, story and presentation are the team's.
