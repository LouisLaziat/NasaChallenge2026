Before you step outside.

Three saved, synthetic scenarios applied to the same demo science stops. Each scenario replans the walk using the engine’s existing condition switches. Radiation uses Curiosity RAD measurements at Gale Crater as a proxy for Jezero.

All three scenarios are invented demonstrations, labelled synthetic: true. GO means the planning thresholds pass. CAUTION calls for review. NO-GO means a solar alert or a failed route budget. Unknown inputs also cause CAUTION (ASSUMPTION). Temperature, pressure and the manual EVA window are shown for context; this version does not model thermal suit limits or enforce a clock window.
