# Marswalk Budget

Marswalk Budget is an educational Mars walking-route planner for an astronaut choosing science stops. On real terrain at Jezero Crater or Gale Crater, it estimates walking time, suit oxygen, radiation dose and science value, with nearby Perseverance or Curiosity photos for context. When dust, a solar event or a heavy sample kit changes the budget, it replans, marks where returning home would require the oxygen reserve, and suggests the most science that still fits. A console, field tablet and helmet display carry the same plan. Published data, geographic proxies and team assumptions are identified, including archival weather. We want to make the trade-off between exploration and getting home understandable and visible.
